# Payments Refunds and Policies

## Payment Flow

Bookings are created first, then payment is completed from Pay Now. Payment amount must match the backend price breakdown. The total includes base fare, insurance, platform fee, chauffeur fee when selected, coupon discount, and other booking amounts.

## Refunds

Refund depends on cancellation time before pickup. Earlier cancellations receive higher refunds. Refunds are credited to the customer wallet when applicable. Security deposit refund depends on payment and trip completion conditions.

## Insurance

Customers choose insurance before booking. Insurance affects the booking total and coverage level. Basic, Standard, and Platinum plans have different percentage rates.

## Chauffeur

Chauffeur service is available for supported cars when selected. Chauffeur fee is calculated per day. Bikes are self-ride and do not use chauffeur service.
