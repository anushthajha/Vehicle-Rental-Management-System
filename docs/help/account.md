# Account Registration Help

## Create an Account

New users create an account from Register. Choose the correct role during registration: Customer to book vehicles, or Vehicle Manager to list and manage vehicles. Enter name, email, phone when required, password, and submit the form. After registration, log in from Login.

Customers must complete KYC before booking a vehicle. Vehicle managers may need admin approval before they can fully operate.

Steps:
1. Open Register.
2. Choose Customer or Vehicle Manager.
3. Enter the required account details.
4. Submit registration.
5. Log in from Login.
6. Complete KYC if you are a customer who wants to book.

## Login

Existing users sign in from Login with their registered credentials. After login, the dashboard and available navigation depend on the user role.

## Choose the Correct Role

Choose Customer if you want to search, book, pay for, track, and review vehicles. Choose Vehicle Manager if you want to add vehicles, manage bookings, handle active trips, and receive earnings. Admin accounts are platform operations accounts and are not part of normal public registration.

## Become a Manager

To become a vehicle manager, register with the Vehicle Manager role. Enter your account details, submit registration, and log in after the account is created. Vehicle manager accounts may need admin approval before they can fully use manager features.

After approval, open the Manager Panel to add vehicles, manage bookings, block unavailable dates, track active trips, view earnings, and request payouts.

Steps:
1. Open Register.
2. Choose Vehicle Manager.
3. Enter the required account details.
4. Submit registration.
5. Log in from Login.
6. Wait for admin approval if the account is pending.
7. Open Manager Panel after approval.
