# Customer Workflow FAQ

## Search Vehicles By City

Customers can search vehicles by city from the landing page search, Browse Vehicles, city pages, or SigBot. Bengaluru can also be typed as Bangalore or BLR. Search results can be filtered by category, type, price, fuel, transmission, seats, rating, and features.

## Book Cars Bikes And Travellers

SigFleet supports different vehicle types such as cars, bikes, and travellers when managers have listed and admins have approved them. Cars may offer self-drive or chauffeur depending on listing support. Bikes are self-ride only and do not use chauffeur service.

## Choose Dates And Time

Customers must select pickup and return date-time before booking. The selected duration must follow the vehicle's minimum trip hours and maximum trip days. If a vehicle is unavailable for the selected range, choose another date or vehicle.

## Vehicle Detail Page

The vehicle detail page shows images, make, model, price, location, features, description, availability, manager information, reviews, and the booking widget. Customers book from this page after selecting dates and insurance.

## Insurance Selection

Insurance is selected before booking. Basic has a lower rate, Standard is the default balanced option, and Platinum provides broader coverage. The selected insurance amount appears in the price breakdown.

## Security Deposit

Security deposit is shown in the booking price area as a refundable amount. It is separate from the rental price and helps protect managers from damages or disputes.

## Active Trip Tracking

During an active trip, customers can open Track Rental to view trip status and support options. Emergency or trip issues should be raised from Help & Support.

## Booking History

Completed and past bookings appear in My Bookings or Rental History. Customers can view old trip details, payment amounts, and write reviews for completed trips.

## Wishlist

Customers can save vehicles to Wishlist. Wishlist helps compare vehicles and quickly return to interesting listings before booking.

## Coupons

Coupons can be entered during booking or payment when available. Coupons may have minimum booking amount, expiry, usage limit, or eligibility rules.

## Notifications

Customers receive notifications for booking, payment, support, wallet, and system updates. Use the bell icon to read, mark as read, view all, or delete notifications.

## Account Settings

Customers can update profile information, contact details, and account preferences from Account Settings or Profile.
