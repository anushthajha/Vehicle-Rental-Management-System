# Customer Help

## Book a Vehicle

Customers can book a vehicle from Browse Vehicles or by using SigBot. Open Browse Vehicles, select the city, pickup date, return date, and preferred vehicle type. Open a vehicle, check the availability message, review the price breakdown, choose insurance, and click Book Now. After booking is created, complete payment from the Pay Now page.

Steps:
1. Go to Browse Vehicles.
2. Select city, pickup date, return date, and vehicle type.
3. Open a vehicle details page.
4. Check availability and price breakdown.
5. Choose insurance and rental type.
6. Click Book Now.
7. Complete payment from Pay Now.

Customers can also book conversationally using SigBot from the customer dashboard. SigBot can search vehicles, calculate price, confirm booking, and guide the customer to payment.

## Complete KYC

Customers must complete KYC before booking. Go to Customer Panel, open KYC Verification, fill Aadhaar and driving licence details, upload required documents, and submit. Admin approval is required before booking is allowed.

Steps:
1. Open Customer Panel.
2. Go to KYC Verification.
3. Enter identity and licence details.
4. Upload documents.
5. Submit and wait for approval.

## Documents Needed

Customers need Aadhaar details, driving licence details, and document images for KYC verification. Upload the required Aadhaar and driving licence documents from Customer Panel -> KYC Verification. KYC must be approved by an admin before the customer can book a vehicle.

Steps:
1. Open Customer Panel.
2. Go to KYC Verification.
3. Enter Aadhaar details.
4. Enter driving licence details.
5. Upload the required document images.
6. Submit for admin approval.

## View and Manage Bookings

Customers can view bookings from My Bookings. Booking details show vehicle information, dates, payment status, trip status, insurance, security deposit, and manager details. Active trips can be tracked from Track Rental.

Steps:
1. Open My Bookings.
2. Select a booking.
3. View booking status and details.
4. Use Track Status for active trips.

## Cancel a Booking

Customers can cancel eligible bookings from the booking details page or by asking SigBot. The cancellation flow shows a refund preview before final confirmation. Refund depends on how early the customer cancels before pickup. Eligible refunds are credited to the wallet.

Steps:
1. Open My Bookings.
2. Select the booking.
3. Choose Cancel Booking.
4. Review refund preview.
5. Confirm cancellation only if the refund details are acceptable.

## Payment and Wallet

After booking, customers complete payment from the Pay Now page. Supported methods include card, UPI, net banking, and wallet. Wallet balance, added money, refunds, and transactions are visible from Customer Panel -> Wallet.

Steps:
1. Open Pay Now from booking success or My Bookings.
2. Select payment method.
3. Complete payment.
4. Check Wallet for balance and refund history.

## Reviews

After a completed trip, customers can write a review from My Bookings or Reviews. Reviews help future customers understand vehicle quality and manager service.

## Support

Customers can create a support ticket from Help & Support. Choose the issue category, describe the problem, attach files if needed, and submit. Staff replies appear in the same ticket thread.
